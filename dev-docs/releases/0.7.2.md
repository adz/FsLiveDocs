## FsLiveDocs 0.7.2

### Fixed

- Source parameter names are recovered by binding identity (name, enclosing module or type, and arity) rather than by an exact line match. An assembly-backed symbol whose reported location falls on its XML documentation, or on its enclosing type's or module's documentation, no longer produces a false `unnamed-parameter` warning, and same-named overloads and neighbouring bindings resolve to the correct declaration.
